
# Task must be complete

You must create all pages that a Service Provider will have access to on the platform. Below are the permissions and capabilities of this role, followed by detailed specifications for each page.

### Service Provider Permissions:

- View and edit their own profile information
- Add, update, and delete services they offer
- Receive and manage orders from investors
- Track order status (pending → in-progress → completed)
- View statistics about their performance and earnings
- Access financial summaries of their service orders

1. Profile page:
   This page displays the service provider's public and private information. The provider can view their details, see all services they offer, check their platform rating, and update their information directly from this page.
2. Managem services pages:
   This page allows the service provider to add new services, as well as update and delete their existing services. Providers can control which services are active and visible to investors.
3. Manageing recived order:
   This page enables the service provider to manage orders that come from investors. The provider can view order details, update order status, and communicate with investors. Orders are categorized by status: Pending, In Progress, and Completed (Well Done).
4. Stats page:
   This page displays a summary of everything related to the provider's business, including order statistics, service performance, and payment traffic. It helps providers understand their business health at a glance.
5. financila page:
   This page displays a summary of the financial transactions related to the provider's service orders. It includes earnings, payments received, pending payments, and transaction history.
